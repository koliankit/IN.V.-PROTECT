# Target User Personas & UX Requirements

Sangyan AI Investor Shield is engineered around five primary vulnerable demographics:

## 1. First-Time Investors
- **Profile**: Novice retail participants opening their first demat/brokerage account or looking for capital growth.
- **Vulnerabilities**: Unfamiliarity with regulatory frameworks, realistic market return expectations, and statutory intermediary registration.
- **UX Requirement**: Clear, jargon-free explanations distinguishing normal market risk from fraudulent guarantees.

## 2. Young Social-Media Users
- **Profile**: Digital natives consuming content on Instagram, Telegram, YouTube Shorts, and WhatsApp.
- **Vulnerabilities**: Susceptible to viral "finfluencers", fake screenshots of high trading profits, and "VIP" pump-and-dump groups.
- **UX Requirement**: Fast screenshot/image upload, visual indicator tags, and unpackaging of social engineering tactics.

## 3. Regional-Language Users
- **Profile**: Non-native English speakers communicating in Hindi, Marathi, Hinglish, and other Indian vernaculars.
- **Vulnerabilities**: Deceptive schemes explicitly targeted via localized messaging and fake regional customer care accounts.
- **UX Requirement**: Multilingual UI support, native/Hinglish text handling, and clear vernacular guidance without technical jargon.

## 4. Elderly Users
- **Profile**: Senior citizens, retirees, and pension holders seeking capital preservation or regular income.
- **Vulnerabilities**: High susceptibility to fear-inducing impersonation (fake police/CBI/RBI threats, digital arrest scams, urgent KYC expiration).
- **UX Requirement**: High contrast typography, clear step-by-step guidance, prominent safety helplines (1930), and calm reassurance.

## 5. Citizens with Limited Digital/Financial Literacy
- **Profile**: Individuals with basic smartphone proficiency but little understanding of UPI mechanics, OTP security, or SEBI grievance systems.
- **Vulnerabilities**: Tricked into clicking APK links, sharing OTPs/screen shares, or transferring funds to "mule" bank accounts.
- **UX Requirement**: Prominent security warnings (never share OTP/install remote-access apps), straightforward next-step actions, and direct links to official reporting portals.
