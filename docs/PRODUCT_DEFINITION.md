# Product Identity & Branding Specification

## Product Name
**Sangyan AI Investor Shield**

*Etymology*: "Sangyan" (संज्ञान) is a Sanskrit-derived term prevalent in Hindi and Indian jurisprudence signifying *cognizance, conscious awareness, and informed discernment*. "Investor Shield" defines the protective, defensive nature of the system.

## Design & Branding Philosophy
- **Style**: Academic, trustworthy, minimalist, and research-grade.
- **Audience**: General public, retail investors, students, senior citizens, regulatory evaluators (IIT hackathon level).
- **Prohibited Aesthetics**:
  - No crypto / "to the moon" / Web3 speculative themes.
  - No day-trading / stock ticker / casino neon visual cues.
  - No get-rich-quick or profit-maximization motifs.
- **Approved Palette**:
  - Deep Navy / Slate Blue (Integrity, Trust, Authority)
  - Clean Off-White / Crisp Light Backgrounds
  - Neutral Muted Accents
  - Standardized Alert Tones (Amber/Orange for Warning, Red for High Concern, Green/Blue for Verified/Safe)
- **Voice & Tone**: Objective, calm, precise, evidence-grounded, deferential to statutory regulators.
