# Explicit Product Non-Goals & Boundaries

To preserve regulatory compliance, academic rigor, and user trust, Sangyan AI Investor Shield establishes immutable non-goals:

## 1. No Buy / Sell / Hold Recommendations
The platform strictly refuses to issue actionable investment decisions, trading calls, entry/exit targets, or ratings on individual equities, commodities, derivatives, or debt instruments.

## 2. No Stock Price or Return Prediction
The platform will never estimate future stock prices, target levels, or forecast returns for any asset or market segment.

## 3. No Portfolio Recommendations
The platform does not evaluate user net worth, propose asset allocations, recommend portfolio restructuring, or endorse thematic investment baskets.

## 4. No Speculative Trading Assistance
The platform does not provide intraday tips, options chains strategies, algorithmic execution scripts, or margin trading prompts.

## 5. No Broker or Intermediary Promotion
The platform maintains strict vendor neutrality. It contains no affiliate links, sponsored broker rankings, or promotional campaigns for trading applications.

## 6. No Financial Product Upselling
The platform is free of commercial upsells, paywalled "premium tips", loan/credit promotions, or cross-selling of insurance schemes.

## Enforcement Mechanism
Requests violating these non-goals are intercepted by guardrails and redirected to educational disclaimers and official regulatory verification portals.
