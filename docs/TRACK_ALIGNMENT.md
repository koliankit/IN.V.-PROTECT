# Hackathon Track Alignment: Primary Track

## Primary Track
- **Track Code**: Track A
- **Track Title**: Digital Fraud & Scam Resilience
- **Scope & Alignment**:
  - Detection and prevention of digital investment scams, social engineering tricks, fraudulent high-yield schemes, and fake trading application downloads.
  - Empowering citizens and retail investors against cyber-enabled financial fraud.
  - Adherence to guidance and advisories from Indian regulators (SEBI, RBI) and cyber defense agencies (I4C, CERT-In).

## Secondary Capability
- **Track Code**: Track E
- **Track Title**: Misinformation & Content Literacy
- **Integrated Architecture Principle**:
  - *No Split Products*: Misinformation analysis is embedded directly into the core investor-safety pipeline.
  - *Content Literacy Support*: Deceptive claims, doctored screenshots, deepfaked regulatory endorsements, and out-of-context investment advice are evaluated as fraud vectors.
  - *Educational Guidance*: Responses explain rhetorical manipulation patterns (e.g. artificial urgency, fake institutional endorsements) to build long-term user discernment.
